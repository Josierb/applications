Essentials to Entrepreneurship (UCD Innovation Academy)

I completed Essentials to Entrepreneurship in the Innovation Academy at UCD, an experiential course designed to help students think and work like entrepreneurs. We spent significant time learning the mindset through lessons (customer discovery, value propositions, business models, branding, and pitching), activities (rapid prototyping, market interviews, and testing assumptions), and structured reflections that pushed us to iterate and improve based on evidence.

We then put those skills into practice with a team project. Our brief was to design a product that is sustainable, ready-to-eat, and attractive to the Irish market. Our team created Spraoi Pots (“Spraoi” means fun in Irish): a premium Greek yogurt paired with granola. The concept focuses on locally sourced ingredients, eco-friendly packaging, and a social impact model where a portion of revenue supports Irish farmers. We explored positioning Spraoi Pots as a protein-forward, grab-and-go option for students, athletes, and health-conscious consumers, with an initial channel strategy of high-end markets and fairs before scaling to retailers.

As part of the project, we developed branding, unit economics, and a go-to-market plan, then delivered a final pitch to the class. You can view the presentation here: Spraoi Pots Pitch (PDF) .

COMP31000: Literature Review

As part of the COMP31000 - Intro to Research & Sci Comm module, I conducted a literature review titled "Predicting Human Actions via Computational Technologies". The review examines how accurately computational models, using sensor data and behavioural traces, can predict individual human actions.

I analyzed 11 papers from the ACM database, focusing on mobility, interaction, and latent traits. The findings indicate that while hybrid models achieve high accuracy in physical tasks, predicting internal psychological states remains a complex challenge.

You can read the full paper here: Download Research Paper (PDF)
